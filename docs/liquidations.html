Reference data only, not financial advice.
All figures on this page come from Binance Futures public endpoints (USDⓈ-M perpetuals) and reflect Binance's own order book and traders only — not the whole market. "Top trader" ratios cover Binance's top 20% of accounts/positions by margin balance, which behaves differently from the retail-heavy "global accounts" ratio, so the two can diverge or even point opposite directions. The liquidation feed is built entirely from Binance's public forceOrder stream while this tab is open (plus whatever was recorded earlier this session, persisted locally on this device) — it is not a full historical record of every liquidation on the exchange. Ratio/open interest/funding figures refresh every 10 minutes; the liquidation feed is real-time via WebSocket and reconnects automatically if it drops.
