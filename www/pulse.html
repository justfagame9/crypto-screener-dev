🐋 Whale TransfersLarge exchange inflow / outflow · Ethereum
Tracks large ETH / USDT / USDC / WBTC moves in or out of a curated set of known Binance, Coinbase & Kraken hot wallets (threshold ≈ $250K). Inflow to an exchange is often a sell signal; outflow to cold storage is often accumulation. Not exhaustive — many whale moves happen off-chain or on other chains.
🐦 Crypto X PulseWhales, exchange founders & VCs to watch
Public feeds, best-effort reliability — not a trading venue.
Whale transfers come directly from Etherscan (requires your own free API key — nothing is sent anywhere but Etherscan and your browser). The curated exchange wallet list is a small, well-known sample, not comprehensive, and doesn't cover other chains. The X/Twitter feed no longer uses Nitter-style mirrors — those were shut down for good after a cease-and-desist from X Corp in August 2026 (the project's own repository was archived days later). It now pulls from X's own public syndication/embed feed instead, the free JSON endpoint that normally powers "Embedded Timelines" on other websites. That feed isn't officially documented for this kind of use and X throttles or empties it for unauthenticated requests on and off, so treat it as best-effort — when it comes back empty, this section falls back to direct profile links instead of failing silently. This page doesn't place trades or hold positions. Nothing here is financial advice.
